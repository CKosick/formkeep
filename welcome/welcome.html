Welcome to FormKeep

FormKeep silently saves what you type into web forms, so a crashed tab, a closed window, or a failed submit never costs you your words. Everything stays on this device.

Step 1: Pin FormKeep to your toolbar

Chrome does not pin new extensions automatically, and FormKeep works quietly in the background, so pin it now while you are thinking about it:

  1. Click the puzzle piece icon at the right end of the Chrome toolbar.
  2. Find FormKeep - Automatic Form Recovery in the list.
  3. Click the pin icon next to it. The FormKeep icon will stay visible next to the address bar, one click away.

Step 2: Prove it works, right now

The box below is a real form field, and FormKeep is already watching it, exactly like any form on the web.

  1. Type a sentence or two in the box, then wait a second while FormKeep saves it.
  2. Reload this page. The box will come back empty, just like after a crash.
  3. Click into the box, then click Restore my text. Your sentence comes back.

Three ways to restore your text, anywhere

  • Toolbar popup: click the FormKeep icon and press Restore on any saved snapshot.
  • Right-click menu: right-click inside a text field and choose "FormKeep: Restore text".
  • Keyboard: press Alt + Shift + R while a text field is focused.

FormKeep never captures passwords, PINs, one-time codes, or payment fields, and nothing you type ever leaves this computer.
